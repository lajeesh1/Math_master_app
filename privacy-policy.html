Privacy Policy for Math Master Quiz

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Developer: Lajeesh

Key Summary: Math Master Quiz is designed as a safe, 100% offline educational mathematics practice application. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data, user identifiers, or location data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Math Master Quiz does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). The app operates entirely locally on your device.

  • No User Account: No registration, login, email address, or phone number is required.
  • No Tracking / Analytics: We do not use third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) to track your in-app actions.
  • Local Storage Only: Quiz scores, selected difficulty levels, and timestamps are saved exclusively in a local SQLite database stored safely on your physical device. This data never leaves your device.

2. Permissions

Math Master Quiz does not request any sensitive Android device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, media, or external storage.

3. Advertising

Math Master Quiz contains no advertisements. There are no advertising SDKs or third-party ad networks embedded in the app.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because Math Master Quiz is an educational tool suitable for students and children of all ages:

  • We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We do not collect personal information from children under 13 or anyone else.
  • The app is completely safe for children, students, teachers, and parents.

5. Data Deletion

Users have full control over their quiz history. You can permanently delete all stored score history at any time directly within the app by opening the "Score History" screen and tapping the "Clear History" button.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
